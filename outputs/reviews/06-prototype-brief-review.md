# Review: outputs/06-prototype-brief.md

**Verdict: PASS WITH FIXES**

Checked against `data/constraints.md` (re-read 2026-10-19), the CLAUDE.md Never list, `outputs/05-decision-memo.md` and `data/customer-journey.md`. The brief has no statistics to trace. I checked all dates with `date`: 14 Nov 2026 is a Saturday, and 6, 7 and 9 Nov 2028 are Mon, Tue and Thu, so the slot labels are correct.

What holds against the hard rules:
- The reminder goes only to consenting customers or those who tick the box.
- The opt-in is unticked and is not required to see the result.
- The reminder carries the optician's name as sender.
- The interval appears only when the ophthalmologist has set it.
- There is no fear, price or urgency wording.
- Opticians are not asked to do extra work, and there is no CRM integration.
- The flow is limited to non-referral customers, which matches the memo.

## Issues

1. **"Already consented" relies on a consent scope nobody has confirmed yet.** Line 80 / prompt state c: "Optik Lindner will remind you by e-mail around October 2028."
   - Rule: consent (constraints §Consent). Today's checkbox (customer-journey §1) reads "I'd like reminders and offers". It doesn't name a sender, and counter bookings (most bookings at independents) may never have shown it. The memo makes "legal (consent scope)" a week-1 item, so this is unresolved.
   - Fix: label state c "pending legal sign-off on consent scope". Add a note that if legal says booking consent doesn't cover optician-named reminders, state c falls back to showing the screen 3 checkbox.

2. **The re-ask sits on the logged-in report page, not in a message.** Line 24 / line 68: "Reminder opt-in on the same page".
   - Rule: consent may be re-asked "only in a message the customer already receives legitimately, or in person". The page is part of delivering the service, but it is not literally a message.
   - Fix: say explicitly that the report page counts as part of the "your result is ready" service delivery, pending legal confirmation (memo week-1 item). Also note that customers who never log in never see the opt-in, so reach depends on `report_opened`.

3. **The salutation needs data that booking doesn't collect.** Lines 40, 89 and 96, and prompt screens 1 and 5: "Hello Ms Schulz" / "Guten Tag Frau Schulz".
   - Rule: Never invent data. Booking collects name, e-mail, date of birth and language (customer-journey §1), but no title or gender.
   - Fix: use "Hello Renate Schulz" / "Guten Tag Renate Schulz" everywhere, including the prompt.

4. **The minor-findings template is medical advice that the prompt locks in as final copy.** Line 53 / prompt state b: "minor findings that do not require a visit to an eye doctor now".
   - Rule: diagnosis and treatment wording must come only from the ophthalmologist's evaluation, and no fear-based wording. "Do not require a visit … now" is a care recommendation generated by a template, and "now" can suggest a later problem.
   - Fix: keep the [needs medical sign-off] tag inside the prompt too; the prompt currently says "use the text exactly as given" with no caveat. Consider "Dr. M. Weber noted minor findings. Details are in your report." / "Dr. M. Weber hat kleinere Befunde notiert. Details finden Sie in Ihrem Bericht."

5. **One combined state is missing: consented at booking, but the optician hasn't opted in.** Edge cases 3 and 5 (lines 112, 114) and prompt states c and d.
   - Rule: optician's name only with the optician's participation; don't promise a message that won't be sent. Today, state c could show "Optik Lindner will remind you" for an optician that never opted in.
   - Fix: say that state d overrides state c, so neither the checkbox nor the confirmation shows.

6. **The confirmation promises a reminder that may never arrive.** Line 77: "Optik Lindner will e-mail you around October 2028."
   - Rule: the reminder must go in the optician's name. If the optician opts out or closes within 23 months, the reminder can't be sent. Re-sending it as Mirantus would break the own-name rule.
   - Fix: add an edge case: "optician leaves the programme before the reminder date: reminder suppressed, no Mirantus-branded substitute". Soften the copy to "Optik Lindner plans to remind you…" / "Optik Lindner wird Sie voraussichtlich … erinnern".

7. **The reminder repeats the interval outside the report.** Lines 90 and 97: "Dr. M. Weber recommended your next screening around November 2028."
   - Rule: an interval is a medical statement and "must come from the ophthalmologist's evaluation, not from marketing copy". It is attributed correctly, but it now sits in a reminder message, not in the report.
   - Fix: add this sentence to the medical/legal sign-off list. A safer fallback: "the date noted in your report: November 2028".

8. **Reply-to is not specified.** Screen 5, line 84: "From: 'Optik Lindner'".
   - Rule: no required extra work for opticians. If replies land in the store inbox, staff have work they never signed up for.
   - Fix: add "Reply-to: none or the Mirantus service address. Questions are answered via the booking page. Opticians receive no replies unless they choose to."

9. **Names and addresses are not clearly fictional to test participants.** Lines 13–14: "Optik Lindner, Hauptstraße 12, 04109 Leipzig"; "Dr. M. Weber".
   - Rule: names must be clearly fictional, and no real-world figures. 04109 is a real Leipzig postcode, and "Optik Lindner" and "Dr. M. Weber" may match real businesses or doctors. Participants come from three real independents (memo), and the screens never say the data is invented.
   - Fix: add a persistent label to the prompt: "Prototype – fictional example / Prototyp – fiktives Beispiel". Use an obviously invented address such as "Musterstraße 12, Musterstadt", or at least drop the real postcode.
